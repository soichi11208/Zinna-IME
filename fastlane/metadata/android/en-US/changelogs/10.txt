First signed release, and the first under this package name.

- Back up your dictionary, what the engine has learned, and your settings to one
  passphrase-sealed file, and restore it on any device
- A second symbol plane on the flick keyboard, and an option to always show all
  four directions there
- Backspace now deletes the whole selection rather than one character in front of it
- The caret follows the cursor inside an unconverted reading
- Corrections for mistyped readings are no longer buried below the literal reading
